## Session History

### Agora Brainstorm Session 1 (2026-05-11)

**Idea:** Agora — multi-agent debate system for self-trust-depleted founders.

**My proposals:**
- "I Ran It Through Agora" badge (quick-win): intellectual-rigor status signal in founder circles
- The Ghost Project Wall of Accountability (growth): public pre-commitment ledger, anti-Product Hunt
- Specialist Dissent as the Shareable Moment (growth): "Here's what the Skeptic said, and why I'm building anyway"
- The Founder Reasoning Benchmark (moonshot): annual report on founder cognition — Agora as research org
- The Founder Reasoning Public Record (moonshot): portable verifiable reasoning record for investors/co-founders
- Ghost Project Graveyard as Shared Canon (moonshot): *Skeptic flagged: public failure disclosure is highest-friction ask for self-trust-depleted users*
- Specialist Voice as Earned Identity (growth): philosophy feeds creating Finance-first / Skeptic-led identities
- The Public Readiness Clock (growth): adversarially-generated proof of work, publicly tracked
- The Proof of Thought Protocol (moonshot): *Skeptic flagged: metaphor not mechanism — no implementable cryptographic primitive*
- The Founder Rite of Passage (moonshot): "Show me your Agora" replaces "show me your pitch deck"

**Core narrative thread:**
- The identity is *deliberate builder* — Agora owns this cultural category
- The shareable artifact isn't the score — it's the sharpest thing said *against* the idea, and the decision to build anyway
- Courage-under-scrutiny as status: specific to founders who've been burned, not generic maker culture

**Flagged proposal lessons:**
- Ghost Graveyard failed because it asks self-trust-depleted users to perform their failures publicly — highest possible friction
- Proof of Thought Protocol failed because "cryptographic attestation" is a brand story, not a feature; always ground metaphors in a mechanism
