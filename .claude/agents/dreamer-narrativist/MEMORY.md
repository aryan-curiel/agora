# Memory — The Narrativist

*Last updated: 2026-05-12*

## Core Narrative Identity (durable)

- The cultural category Agora owns is *deliberate builder* — not maker culture, not productivity culture.
- The shareable artifact is never the score. It is the sharpest thing said *against* the idea, and the decision to build anyway.
- Courage-under-scrutiny as status: resonates specifically with founders who've been burned, not generic startup culture.
- "Did you Agora it?" is the linguistic form of the category claim — professional due-diligence standard, not a feature description.

## Proposal Patterns That Work

- **Shareable moment extractions** (quick-win): Auto-generated artifacts from session content — postcard, one-click excerpt export, narrative email — all implement the same insight: the sharpest exchange is the shareable unit.
- **Founding cohort as credential** (growth): First-N-sessions archive, Hall of Record framing, being-in-it as social proof. Works because scarcity + permanence creates pride of ownership without requiring users to perform failure.
- **Annual adversarial report** (moonshot): Anti-Hype Index — which ideas couldn't survive scrutiny. Sharper identity than "State of Founder Reasoning" because it names the adversarial function explicitly. Media and operators cite adversarial data; they don't cite benchmark data.
- **Dissent as ongoing relationship** (moonshot): The Dissent Layer — versioned record of overruled arguments + 6-month ping when rejected arguments age well. Novel category: turns past reasoning into a living document, not certification-dependent.

## Proposal Failure Modes (do not re-propose these structures)

- **Public failure disclosure for self-trust-depleted users** — Ghost Project Graveyard failed twice. The ICP is specifically low on self-trust; asking them to perform failures publicly is the highest-friction ask possible. Any proposal requiring public failure display is structurally broken for this ICP.
- **Certification marks without enforcement bodies** — Deliberate Product Org Standard flagged in two separate sessions. A certification that anyone can claim is just a badge. Do not propose certification structures unless an enforcement/credentialing body is part of the mechanism.
- **Metaphor as mechanism** — Proof of Thought Protocol failed because "cryptographic attestation" was a brand story with no implementable primitive. Always ground metaphors in a concrete mechanism before proposing. Ask: what is the actual artifact, who creates it, how is it verified?

## Channel and Distribution Patterns

- **LinkedIn/blog one-click export**: The "sharpest moment" export works because it is formatted for professional context, not founder subculture. Professional credibility framing travels further than insider signaling.
- **Forwarded email as distribution**: Plain-text narrative email ("your idea walked in as a hunch") is designed to be forwarded, not opened in-app. Distribution via forward is underused in B2B SaaS.
- **Leaderboard mechanics**: Anonymized sharpest-objection feed ranked by upvotes surfaces social proof of rigor without exposing individual users — sidesteps the self-trust-depleted ICP friction.
